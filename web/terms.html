I am Aryan Kumar Singh, and I built Glidepath as a research demo. It shows a replay of held-out test flights and the results of a pilot study.

It is not for operational use. Do not use it to plan flights, staff, stands or any other real operation.

Flight data comes from The OpenSky Network and is used under the OpenSky terms of use for research. See opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use.

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, built on OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data, copyright OpenStreetMap contributors.
