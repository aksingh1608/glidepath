Privacy

This site does not use cookies. It does not track visitors and does not collect personal data.

The site loads the map library from unpkg.com and map tiles from tiles.openfreemap.org. Those servers receive your IP address as part of any web request.

The flight data shows aircraft, not people. It comes from public ADS-B broadcasts collected by The OpenSky Network and is used for research.
